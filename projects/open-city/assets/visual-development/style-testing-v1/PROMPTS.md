# Prompt blocks — style testing v1

Generation mode: Codex built-in `image_gen` with local image references. CLI fallback
не использовался.

## Общая система

```text
OpenCity Color-Breath; adult urban satirical 2D comic; simple readable anatomy;
stable confident warm-dark contour; restrained flat cel shading; one even matte
cream-paper grain layer; everyday post-Soviet environment; cool light gray-blue
and gray-teal surroundings; warm natural skin; small local technology accents.
Fatalism is in the premise, not in dark grading. Keep the image airy and lightly
absurd. No neo-glitch, scanlines, registration bars, global cyan-magenta glow,
neon cyberpunk, propaganda, grim war mood, photorealism, 3D or anime.
```

## Light Deadpan

```text
Airy soft daylight, relaxed ordinary bodies, neutral mouths and dry recognition
of routine absurdity. Do not make the premise visually bleak.
```

## Light Warm

```text
Preserve composition, camera, crop, identities, proportions, objects, line weight,
detail density and paper grain. Change only lighting temperature and exposure,
facial acting, eye direction and minimal posture. Slightly warmer natural faces,
relaxed shoulders, small mutual glances and restrained half-smiles. No broad smiles,
sentimentality, cuteness, sitcom cheer or orange global grading.
```

## Контроль персонажей

```text
Flepik: exactly two continuous closed teal ribbon loops attached directly to the
top cap at both bottom ends. No stem, rod, lollipop circle or detached ring.

NEYRA and Max: same abstraction and line density as Danya and Семёновна. Reduce
facial planes, hair strands, garment seams and heroic fashion rendering.

Grisha: exactly two rotor rings, half-lidded cream eyes, worn teal body, one orange
cargo cassette with exact dark stencil text "CITY-005". No ribbon loops.

Mayor: no body, face, eyes, screen head, suit, hologram or mascot. Presence only
through synchronized ordinary interfaces and the service mark: three horizontal
neuro-green lines of different lengths above one thin ring.
```
