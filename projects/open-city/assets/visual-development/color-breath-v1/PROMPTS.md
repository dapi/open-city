# Color-Breath approval pack — prompt manifest

Статус всех результатов: `candidate_for_showrunner_review`.

Режим генерации: Codex built-in `image_gen`, без финального леттеринга.

## Общие роли входных изображений

- Image 1 — главный стилевой референс: ранняя десятикадровая раскадровка.
- Image 2 — вспомогательный стилевой референс: OpenCity Visual DNA. Использовать
  линию, печатную фактуру и кремовую основу; игнорировать весь текст и опечатки.
- Image 3 и далее — только источники идентичности персонажей. Не переносить из них
  тёмный фон, неон, cyan/magenta rim light, glitch, scanlines или цифровой шум.

## Общий визуальный блок

Use case: illustration-story

Style/medium: OpenCity Color-Breath, adult urban satirical 2D comic; simple readable
anatomy; confident warm dark-brown ink contour; soft cel shading; one even layer of
matte cream-paper print grain; modest illustrated realism; recognizably everyday
post-Soviet city rather than futuristic spectacle.

Color palette: warm cream paper and speech bubbles; cool desaturated gray-blue and
gray-teal environment; warm natural skin; dark olive graphite; one small neural-green
technology accent; brick-orange only for warning; muted plum only as a character
identifier. No pure-black or pure-white dominant fields.

Constraints: no readable text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark or signature; no
neo-glitch; no acid colors; no global cyan-magenta glow; no dark neon cyberpunk; no
registration bars; no digital noise; no decorative scanlines; no bloom; no chromatic
aberration; no photorealism; no painterly concept art; no 3D; no glossy plastic; no
anime; no propaganda-poster treatment.

## 01 — style calibration page

Asset type: Color-Breath visual calibration page for Showrunner approval.

Primary request: Create one coherent vertical 2:3 comic page divided into four clean
panels, testing the target style without telling a finished story. Show: an overcast
residential courtyard with panel apartment blocks; a mundane municipal waiting room;
a warm human close-up beside a small neural-green terminal; and an ensemble reaction
shot with Danya, Flepik and Grandma Marina, while the same small neural-green status
appears on two separate everyday interfaces. Technology stays small and ordinary.
Include at most two empty cream speech bubbles with dark outlines.

Composition/framing: four panels with calm gutters; mix wide, medium and close shot;
human action reads before devices; leave safe margins for a 4:5 crop.

Identity constraints: preserve the three supplied character silhouettes, outfits and
faces, but translate them completely into Color-Breath. The Нейро-мэр is not a fourth
body in the scene: show only its coordinated effects. No additional named characters.
