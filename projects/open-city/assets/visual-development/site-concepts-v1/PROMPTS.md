## Общий блок

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high-fidelity desktop website homepage concept, 16:10 landscape
Product: OpenCity / «Город Нейросеть», a satirical comic studio, fictional city hall, and entrance to a municipal information system.
Common information architecture: top navigation with «Выпуски», «Город», «Персонажи», «Новости»; a prominent current comic card labeled «Выпуск 005»; city system status; latest studio news.
Art direction shared across the project: adult illustrated satire in a recognizable post-Soviet near-future city; confident dark-brown outlines, soft cel shading, matte paper grain; cool gray-teal environment, warm cream paper and human warmth, restrained neural green and brick-orange accents.
Typography: bold readable Cyrillic grotesque plus restrained monospace for system data.
Constraints: show the whole coherent homepage above the fold, polished professional web design, strong grid and hierarchy, accessible contrast, practical navigation, believable as a real website. Keep visible Cyrillic text limited to the exact labels specified above plus «OpenCity» and «Система работает». No other readable text.
Avoid: generic SaaS landing page, global neon cyberpunk, cyan-magenta glow, decorative glitch, scanlines, acid colors, glossy 3D, photorealism, illegible text, excessive dashboards, watermark.
```

## A — Муниципальная операционная система

```text
Concept A — «Муниципальная операционная система».
Primary request: design the homepage as an official civic desktop operating system subtly occupied by a creative comic studio.
Composition: modular overlapping but orderly application windows; a warm cream comic issue window is the main focus; smaller windows show municipal status, studio news and city services; a compact terminal command strip integrated at the bottom.
Mood: bureaucratic, humane, witty, slightly rebellious. Municipal form stamps and window controls are functional, not decorative clutter.
Make this clearly the balanced flagship direction: roughly 70% official system, 30% creative studio intervention.
```

## B — Цифровая мэрия

```text
Concept B — «Цифровая мэрия».
Primary request: design the homepage as a credible public-service portal for a city governed by an AI mayor.
Composition: restrained civic masthead, large editorial hero for the latest comic, clean service tiles, a narrow live city-status ribbon, structured municipal information hierarchy.
Mood: calm, institutional and trustworthy at first glance, with subtle absurd satirical details. More editorial and architectural than terminal-like.
Make it elegant, spacious, highly usable, and believable as the official website of a post-Soviet city in the near future.
```

## C — Панк-бюллетень поверх системы

```text
Concept C — «Панк-бюллетень поверх системы».
Primary request: design the homepage as a collision between a strict municipal information portal and an independent underground comic editorial layer.
Composition: rigid official document grid underneath; cream paper cutouts, brick-orange stamps, handwritten-looking marks without readable handwriting, tape, registration numbers and bold editorial framing layered over it; latest comic dominates.
Mood: energetic civic dissent, handmade editorial zine, witty rather than aggressive.
Keep the collage controlled and legible. The interface must still work as a real website; no grunge overload and no punk clichés like skulls.
```

## D — Город как файловая система

```text
Concept D — «Город как файловая система».
Primary request: design the homepage as an explorable digital map of OpenCity where districts behave like folders and applications.
Composition: stylized isometric-but-flat illustrated city map as the central navigation surface; city hall, studio, archive and residential blocks become clear clickable modules; a side panel presents «Выпуск 005»; top navigation remains conventional.
Mood: inviting, curious, civic and systemic; the user feels they are entering the city itself.
Use flat 2D illustration rather than glossy 3D. Preserve clear web hierarchy and avoid making it look like a game screen.
```
